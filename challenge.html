Adept Trials

Work together to progress through the Survivor roster.

  • Escape a Trial to advance to the next Survivor.
  • Being sacrificed costs you progress.
  • Three consecutive sacrifices ends the run.
  • Complete your Survivor column to become an Escapist.
  • Escapists may play as any Survivor, but must equip one unique perk from each remaining player's current Survivor and one unique perk from the Survivor they finished their column with.
  • As an Escapist, your goal is to help the remaining players escape and complete their columns.
  • Complete the Trial by having all four players become Escapists.
